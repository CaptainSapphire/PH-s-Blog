# First Month Back: A college sophomore month recap
The college semester has started for many, including for myself, as I enter my second year! 

## Classes
I have 5 classes this semester. Rather, 4 classes and a lab. 
- **Computer Science, Architectures and Structures:** A fully online, asynchronous class. We are given ample time to complete our assignments, and so far it has been interesting. 
- **Engineering, Introduction to MATLAB:** I am enjoying MATLAB so far, as it is a class heavy on programming; it is similar to R, a language I am familiar with and actively enjoy. Most of all, I am enjoying seeing the real-world applications of the mathematics problems we are given, specifically via real-world type of examples given by the professor.
-  **Physics, Calculus Based:** This is one of my more challenging classes, since I often struggle with setting up word problems as models; however, if I am able to overcome this hurdle, the process of solving these problems are much simpler.
- **Calculus 3:** A lot of my associates tell me this is a fun class, with a lot of puzzle solving. So far, we have studied vectors and the 3d space, which I've been informed is "setting up for the fun stuff." I look forward to what this class has in store for the rest of the semester. 

## Clubs
- **Girls Who Code:** The club I started last year is active once more! We have had our first two meetings, and we will have our third meeting this Thursday. The first meeting was a introduction to the officers and club, and the second was a project showcase. The third meeting is "how to prepare for a hackathon;" the reason we are hosting that meeting is because we are hosting a hackathon at the college. The Moorpark Codefest, a beginner friendly hackathon. [Find more details here!](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcPcqacPSit/?utm_source=ig_web_button_share_sheet&stkn=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==)
- **Art and Mental Health Club:** We had our first meeting of the semester. We introduced our officers, recapped our previous year, and had a doodling activity. While turn out was a little low, I look forward to seeing what new faces join us post-club rush. Our next meeting is going to be a Makerspace collaboration on October 1st. We will also be attending the Moorpark Health Fair to talk about art in relation to mental health benefits. Looking ward to this semester. :)

## Other
Beyond school and clubs, I still work part-time. I am grateful for the opportunity to work in such an environment. 

## Conclusion
While the start of the semester has certainly been busy, I am so thankful to have these opportunities to learn and grow. Thank you for reading!!
